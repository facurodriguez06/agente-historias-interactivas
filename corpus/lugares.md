
# Locations in the Narrative World

## Entrance Hall
This is the main entrance to the house. It contains a stopped clock, a coat rack, and a staircase leading to the upper floor. From here, the user can access the living room and a hallway leading to the library. The exterior door may become stuck due to the wind.

## Library
The library is located at the end of the ground-floor hallway. It contains bookshelves, a desk, drawers, and a large window overlooking the lake. The desk may contain relevant notes, but discovering them requires the user to investigate it. One bookshelf shows recent signs of having been moved.

## Greenhouse
The greenhouse is accessible from the living room through a glass door. It contains empty flowerpots, gardening tools, and a locked metal box. The sound of rain is particularly loud against its roof.

## Upstairs Study
The study is located at the top of the staircase. Its door is usually locked, and there is an opening mechanism that the player must discover during the investigation. Its interior must not be described as having been visited if the user has not yet managed to enter.
