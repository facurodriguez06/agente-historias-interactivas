## Consistent World Rules
The house has intermittent electricity, frequent rain, and roads that can be difficult to travel on. Locked doors require a key, a combination, or a justified action to open them; they cannot be opened simply for the convenience of the plot. The user's actions must have persistent consequences. The narrator cannot claim that the user picked up an object unless the user actually chose to do so.

## Scope of the Mystery
Each story may explore a different mystery within the residence and its lakeside grounds: disappeared documents, a resident or guest who vanished suddenly leaving belongings behind, a missing heirloom or painting, or unexplained signals and sounds coming from the lake, garden, or upper floor. Clues can be discovered in different orders, and stories do not all need to begin in the same room. This document does not establish a predetermined culprit: the ending must be constructed consistently with what the user actually investigated, without suddenly revealing information that has not yet been discovered.
